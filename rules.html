      Club Rules & Privacy

      Our 18+ membership rules, community promise, and privacy details.

      This fantasy romance club is exclusively for adults aged 18 and older. Do not register if you are under 18.

      Membership requires an invitation, an adult declaration, and owner approval. False declarations and abusive behavior may lead to removal. Mark spoilers, respect fellow readers, and do not post private information, threats, harassment, or unlawful content.

      Privacy

      We store your username, display name, password hash, adult declaration, approval status, profile, messages, uploaded photos/GIFs/videos, discussions, reading progress, votes, and RSVPs. Date of birth is checked during signup and is not stored. Other approved members can see your display name, profile, and contributions. The owner can moderate and remove accounts and shared media. Uploaded images may contain camera/location metadata; remove that information before sharing. External links open other websites, whose privacy policies apply. Sign-in attempt IP addresses are retained for up to 15 minutes. Sessions use a necessary sign-in cookie; this code includes no advertising trackers.

      Ask the owner to remove your account or report a suspected underage member. Account removal deletes your contributions from the active database and blocks your old username; backups may retain earlier data until the owner deletes them.
